RPSLS

Rock–Paper–Scissors–Lizard–Spock (RPSLS) is a five-gesture expansion of the classic Rock–Paper–Scissors game, created to break the cycle of endless ties. Each move defeats two others and is beaten by two, adding layers of strategy and unpredictability. The gestures are: Rock crushes Scissors and crushes Lizard; Paper covers Rock and disproves Spock; Scissors cut Paper and decapitate Lizard; Lizard eats Paper and poisons Spock; Spock smashes Scissors and vaporizes Rock. Popularized by "The Big Bang Theory," RPSLS is perfect for tournaments and group play, combining quick decision-making, mind games, and a dash of nerdy fun.
